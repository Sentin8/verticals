Single backlog for the repository-split effort and its follow-ups. The
designs of record are `design/sentin8-repository-split-design.md` (RS) and
`design/sentin8-sdk-dependency-cleanup-design.md` (SDD). The original
monorepo (`../sentin8/`) is read as the source of truth for RS and never
modified; SDD executes against `sentin8.new/` directly.

- **Priority**: everything here is `P0` for this effort (the split is the
  current program). Ordering is by build order (RS §2.5, SDD §3), not
  priority.

- **IDs**: `RS-*` (split), `SD-*` (SDK dependency-direction cleanup +
  dissolution, per SDD). Defect classes fixed in the move are referenced
